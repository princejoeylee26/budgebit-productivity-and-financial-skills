# Energy zones and themes

Read this when labelling plan blocks with zone names or when the user wants a different theme. The theme is only a memorable language for energy. It is a metaphor, not religious instruction. The user picks the theme during onboarding; it is stored in memory as `theme: kami | neutral | <custom>`. Default: `kami`.

## Kami theme (default)

Figures from Japanese mythology (*Kojiki*, 712, and *Nihon Shoki*, 720) and folk tradition, chosen because their stories fit the energy they label.

| Zone | Emoji | Fits | Why this figure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Amaterasu** | ☀ | morning rise: routine, sunlight, morning training | Sun goddess; her return from the cave brings light back to the world |
| **Hachiman** | 🏹 | Peak deep work: one target | Kami of archery and warriors; aim, then release |
| **Benzaiten** | 🎵 | Creative: filming, writing, design, voice | Goddess of music, art, eloquence (one of the Seven Lucky Gods) |
| **Inari** | 🦊 | money and business admin: budgets, bills, invoices, sales | Kami of rice, prosperity and commerce; fox messengers |
| **Susanoo** | ⛈ | storm: low-energy or chaos days, or all-out efforts (race day, launch push) that must be followed by recovery | Storm god, powerful and unruly |
| **Tsukuyomi** | 🌙 | evening wind-down: dinner, night routine, light planning for tomorrow | Moon god |
| **Daruma** | 🎎 | restart after a slip | The Daruma doll that always rights itself (七転び八起き) |

Shallow admin that isn't money can sit in Tsukuyomi or in a short "sweep" block with no kami. Recovery anchors (sleep, meals, family) can be labelled with the zone of their time of day, or plain.

Use at most one kami label per block and don't explain the myth unless asked.

## Custom themes
Users can invent their own (e.g. seasons, sports positions). Store the mapping in memory under `## Preferences`. Always keep the underlying types (Peak, Creative, Shallow, Recovery) the same so rules still apply.
