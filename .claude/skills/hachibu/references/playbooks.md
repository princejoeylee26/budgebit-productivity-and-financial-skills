# Playbooks

Read this for a situation that needs a ready-made pattern. Adapt using memory; the user's own rules outrank these defaults.

## First run without memory
Use only if `hachibu-memory` isn't installed. Otherwise run its onboarding.
1. Pull the last 14 days with `include_done: true`.
2. Ask at most 3 questions: usual wake and sleep times; fixed work hours or shifts; the one goal that matters most this month.
3. Plan today from live data, label the energy windows as "guess".

## Low-energy day (Susanoo · energy ≤2)
- 1 must-do, the smallest that still matters.
- Move every Peak task to the next good day (drafts).
- Keep Recovery: meal, short walk outside (shinrin-yoku), early night.
- One Shallow sweep max, 30 min.
- Tone: shikata ga nai. No guilt.

## Heavy training day
(long run, race, key session, double session)
- Max 1 Peak block, not within ~3 h after the hard session.
- Ma buffer 15–30 min after training for food and shower.
- Creative/on-camera work avoided when visibly tired.
- Early wind-down.

## Launch or deadline week
- Pareto: list the 3–5 launch-critical tasks; everything else is deferred or dropped (the user drops in the app).
- Nemawashi: schedule the conversations (partner, team, family) early in the week.
- Murphy: one empty buffer half-day before the deadline.
- Gaman, with limits: sleep and meals stay fixed.
- Keep training at maintenance if the user's plan allows; never cut sleep.

## Overdue catch-up
- Triage first (workflow 3), new work second.
- Max 3 rescheduled tasks per day, Shallow tasks in sweeps of 4–6.
- Look for the cause: muri (one overloaded day), mura (uneven week), unclear tasks (Kidlin: rewrite the title as a next action).
- Kintsugi: name the repair, not the failure.

## Money-tight week
Use `hachibu-money` if installed.
- `Budgebit:budget_status` for this month; `Budgebit:list_bills` with `due_within_days: 14`.
- Move purchase tasks behind payday or out of the month.
- Suggest no-spend activities for Recovery blocks.
- Never move money; draft only.

## Travel or office day
- Commute is Shallow or Recovery (podcasts, calls, admin on phone), never Peak.
- Ma buffer 15 min after arrival.
- Max 1 Peak block on the day.

## Weekend mode
- Defaults: Saturday light (1 must-do, people first), Sunday review slot. Override with memory.
- Ichigo ichie: family and friends time phone-free.

## Polishing loop (bikeshedding)
- Name it: "Law of triviality, this is the bike shed."
- Timebox 10 min, choose, write the decision in the task description, move to the real task.
- Wabi-sabi: ship the good-enough version.
